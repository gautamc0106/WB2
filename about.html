About SCHOLARLY

Our Mission

SCHOLARLY is a demonstration scholarship application portal built for a Web Technology laboratory assignment. Our mission is to simplify access to scholarship information and provide students with a clean, modern application experience.

Scholarship Categories

  • Merit-Based Scholarships
  • Need-Based Financial Aid
  • Sports Excellence Scholarships
  • Technical Skill Scholarships

Important Notice

This is an academic demonstration project. It does not represent a real scholarship provider, and no applications are submitted to any actual institution. No personal data is stored in a database.
